Commercial Solar

Solar sized to how your business actually uses power

Commercial solar decisions involve more stakeholders and more constraints than a residential install — roof structure, three-phase supply, operating hours and site access all shape the design. We work through these with you before proposing a system.

What we consider

Design factors for commercial sites

  • Roof structure, access and any load limitations
  • Three-phase supply capacity and switchboard condition
  • Daytime operating hours and load profile
  • Site access for installation and ongoing maintenance
  • Staging across multiple buildings or tenancies, where relevant

Process

From enquiry to a commissioned system

Step 1

Project enquiry

Tell us about the site, your electricity usage and what you're trying to achieve.

Step 2

Site assessment

We review recent bills and, where needed, visit site to assess roof, switchboard and supply.

Step 3

Design & proposal

A tailored system design and clear written proposal matched to your site.

Step 4

Install & commission

Licensed installation, DNSP approval and commissioning, with documentation for your records.
