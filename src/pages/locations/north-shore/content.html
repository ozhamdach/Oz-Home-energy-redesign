Typical system sizes

What suits this area

Typical residential systems in this area: 10 kW to 20 kW solar with 10–15 kWh battery storage.

Upper North Shore properties on larger blocks often achieve excellent solar yields thanks to generous north-facing roof planes and relatively low shading. System sizes of 10 kW to 20 kW are common, and battery storage is increasingly popular for evening self-consumption.
