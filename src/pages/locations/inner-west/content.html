Typical system sizes

What suits this area

Typical residential systems in this area: 3.3 kW to 6.6 kW solar, sized to the available unshaded roof area.

Inner West terraces and semis present unique solar challenges: small roof footprints, shared walls and heritage overlays in some streets require careful system design. Micro-inverter and string-with-optimiser configurations often suit these rooftops better than a standard single-string layout.
