Residential Electrical

The electrical work behind a safe, capable home

Solar, batteries and EV chargers all depend on electrical infrastructure that's in good condition. We carry out general residential electrical work — with or without a renewable energy project attached.

What we cover

Electrical work for existing homes

Switchboard upgrades

Replacing old fuse-based boards, adding safety switches, and creating capacity for future solar, battery or EV work. See switchboard upgrades.

Fault-finding & repairs

Tripping circuits, flickering lights or dead powerpoints diagnosed and fixed properly, not just patched.

Safety switches & RCDs

Installing or upgrading residual current devices to current safety requirements.

Lighting & power circuits

New powerpoints, lighting circuits and general wiring for renovations or everyday household needs.

Pre-purchase electrical checks

An independent look at a property's electrical condition before you buy or renovate.

Compliance & certification

Work carried out and certified to Australian Standards, with documentation you can keep on file.

The connected-home angle

Why we look at electrical work differently

Because we also design and install solar, battery and EV systems, we look at general electrical jobs with an eye on what your property might need next — even if that's not part of today's job. If a switchboard replacement now avoids a second visit before a future battery or EV charger, we'll tell you.
