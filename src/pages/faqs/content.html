FAQs

Frequently asked questions

For solar, battery, EV and electrical questions, see the Learning Centre. These are the more practical questions about working with us.

How much does a solar or battery system cost?

Cost depends entirely on system size, equipment selected and your property's specific requirements, so we don't publish generic pricing. You'll receive a tailored, itemised quotation after your assessment and any required site visit.

How long does an installation take?

Most residential solar or battery installations are completed within a day, though larger systems, switchboard upgrades or commercial projects can take longer. Timeframes also depend on DNSP approval, which we lodge on your behalf and which can take one to a few weeks depending on your network area.

Do you offer finance or payment plans?

Finance options depend on your circumstances and the system being quoted, so we talk through what's available as part of your specific quotation rather than advertising general terms here.

What rebates or incentives am I eligible for?

Eligibility and current rebate amounts depend on your circumstances and change over time, so we confirm this as part of your specific quotation rather than advertising a figure that may not apply to you.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — Oz Home Energy holds NSW Electrical Contractor Licence 382607C. Insurance details are available on request.

What areas do you service?

Residential work is focused on Greater Sydney. Suitable solar, battery and commercial projects are considered further afield across NSW. See service areas.

What if something goes wrong after installation?

Submit a service request and our team will follow up. Workmanship and product warranty terms are set out in your specific contract and product documentation.

How do I make a complaint?

See our complaints process for how to raise a concern and what to expect.
