How it fits together

One connected energy system, not four separate purchases

Solar, batteries, EV charging and your home's electrical infrastructure work best when they're planned together — even if you're only adding one of them today. That's the difference between a system that's installed and one that's properly designed.

01 · GENERATE

Solar panels

Convert sunlight into electricity your home or business can use straight away.

02 · STORE

Battery storage

Hold on to what you don't use immediately, so more of it's available in the evening. Backup power during an outage is only available where backup circuits are specifically designed and installed for it.

03 · CHARGE

EV charging

Put stored or generated solar to use charging a vehicle overnight or during the day.

04 · CONTROL

Electrical infrastructure

A switchboard and wiring built to safely carry and manage all of the above, now and as you add to it.

Common questions

Before you get in touch

What size solar system suits my home?

System size is a balance of your roof space and orientation, how much electricity you use during daylight hours, your DNSP-approved export limit, and your budget. We work from your electricity bill and household routine first, rather than sizing purely to "fill the roof."

Can my switchboard support an EV charger or a battery?

Many older switchboards don't have available capacity without an upgrade — this is one of the first things we check, before discussing which equipment to install.

Do I need council or strata approval for solar?

Most standalone houses don't need council approval for a standard rooftop system. Strata properties, heritage-listed homes and some local planning overlays can have extra requirements — we'll flag this during your assessment if it's likely to apply.
