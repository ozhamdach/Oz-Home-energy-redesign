When cleaning actually helps

Most Sydney homes get enough rainfall to keep panels reasonably clean without regular servicing. Cleaning makes the most difference where panels sit under trees, near heavy dust or pollen, or have visible bird droppings concentrated in one area — which is also worth pairing with a look at bird-proofing if birds are nesting under the panels.
