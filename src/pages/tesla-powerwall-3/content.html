Suitability

Is Powerwall 3 suitable for your home?

Whether Powerwall 3 is the right choice depends on your existing or planned solar system, your switchboard and supply arrangement, your electricity usage pattern, and what you actually want backup power to cover. We check all of this at a site assessment rather than recommending a specific product before we've seen your property.

How it fits together

Solar, storage and backup options

Powerwall 3 can be installed alongside new solar or added to some existing systems, depending on your current inverter and switchboard. Backup power during an outage is only available where backup circuits are specifically designed and installed for that purpose — it isn't automatic, and which circuits stay powered is a decision we work through with you before installation, not after.

Growing with your home

Expandable storage

Some households want to start with one unit and add capacity later as their needs change. Whether that's possible for your property, and what it would involve, depends on your specific setup — we'll confirm this as part of your assessment rather than assuming it applies to every installation.

Before we quote

What we check before quoting

  • Existing or planned solar system, inverter brand and compatibility
  • Switchboard condition and available capacity
  • Which circuits you want covered during an outage, if any
  • Your current electricity usage pattern
  • Site access and mounting location

Design, backup coverage and overall suitability are confirmed at your site assessment — not assumed in advance.
