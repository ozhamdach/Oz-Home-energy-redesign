Solar panel bird-proofing & mesh

Birds nesting under solar panels can cause droppings, noise, blocked drainage and, in some cases, damage to wiring. A properly fitted mesh keeps birds out without affecting panel performance or voiding warranties.

A clean, low-profile fit

Mesh is fitted around the perimeter of the panel array, sitting close to the roofline so it's not obtrusive. It's a straightforward add-on to an existing system and doesn't require touching the panels' electrical connections.
