Switchboard Upgrades

Often the first thing that needs to change

Old fuse boards and undersized switchboards are one of the most common reasons a solar, battery or EV project stalls. We replace and upgrade switchboards to a safe, compliant standard with capacity for what you're adding.

Start Your Electrical Enquiry Call 0435 336 336

Signs an upgrade is due

What we're usually looking at

  • Ceramic fuses instead of circuit breakers
  • No safety switches (RCDs) fitted
  • Visible scorching, rust or loose wiring
  • No spare capacity for a solar inverter, battery or EV circuit
  • A single-phase board where a planned upgrade needs three-phase

Not every older switchboard needs full replacement — sometimes targeted work is enough. We'll tell you which applies once we've had a look.

What's included

A switchboard upgrade, done properly

Assessment

Existing board, wiring condition and available capacity checked against what you need now and may need later.

Design for what's next

If solar, battery or EV charging is on your radar, capacity for it is built in rather than requiring a second upgrade.

Licensed replacement

New switchboard installed to current Australian Standards, including safety switches and correctly rated circuit breakers.

Labelling & documentation

Circuits clearly labelled, with compliance documentation provided for your records.

Minimal disruption

Power is off for the shortest practical window, scheduled around your household.

Ongoing support

A point of contact if you have questions about your new board down the track.
