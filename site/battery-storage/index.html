Battery Storage

Use more of the solar you already generate

A battery stores the solar you're not using during the day so it's available in the evening. Backup power during a blackout may be available where suitable backup functionality and essential circuits are specifically designed and installed. We install new battery systems and retrofit batteries to existing solar.

Retrofit or new

Adding a battery to existing solar

If you already have solar, the first question is whether your inverter and switchboard can support a battery, and whether it makes sense as a DC-coupled or AC-coupled addition. We check the brand, age and condition of your existing system before recommending an approach.

If you're starting from nothing, solar and battery can be designed and installed together — see solar & battery upgrades if you have an older solar system that also needs attention.

Common questions

Battery storage, answered plainly

Is a battery right for my existing solar?

It depends on how much solar you currently export unused, your evening electricity use, and your existing inverter's compatibility. A short assessment of your bill and system details usually answers this before any site visit.

What should I know about backup power?

Most battery systems don't automatically back up your whole home — backup is usually a defined set of essential circuits, set up deliberately during installation. It's worth deciding what should stay on before the system is designed.

Will a battery work with my current inverter?

Some inverters have a matching battery from the same manufacturer; others need an AC-coupled battery with its own inverter. We'll confirm what your specific system supports before quoting.
