Complaints

Our complaints process

If something hasn't met your expectations, we want to know. This page sets out how to raise a concern and what happens next.

Step 1

Tell us directly

Call 0435 336 336, email admin@ozhomeenergy.com.au, or submit a service request describing the issue. Most concerns are resolved quickly through direct contact.

Step 2

We acknowledge and investigate

We'll confirm receipt of your complaint and look into what happened, including a site visit if relevant.

Step 3

We respond with an outcome

We'll come back to you with our findings and, where applicable, a proposed remedy.

Step 4

If you're not satisfied

If you feel your complaint hasn't been resolved appropriately, you can contact the relevant ombudsman for independent advice on your options.
