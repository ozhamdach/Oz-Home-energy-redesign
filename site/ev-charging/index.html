Charge your EV at home, safely and on your terms

What we check first

A proper assessment, not a guess

Most homes don't need a switchboard upgrade for a single EV charger. Some do — particularly older boards, or homes that already run solar, a pool pump or ducted air conditioning. We check your available capacity, your vehicle's charging requirements and where the charger needs to go before recommending equipment, so you're not sold a unit — or an upgrade — you don't need.

Common questions

EV charging, answered plainly

Can my switchboard support an EV charger?

Many older switchboards don't have available capacity for a dedicated EV circuit without an upgrade. We check this before recommending a charger, and explain what's involved if an upgrade is needed. See switchboard upgrades.

Single-phase or three-phase charging?

Most homes run single-phase power, which suits standard home EV charging speeds. Three-phase supply allows faster charging where available and where the vehicle supports it. We'll confirm what your property has during the assessment.

Can I charge from solar during the day?

Some chargers support solar-aware or scheduled charging so you draw more from your own system and less from the grid. Whether this suits you depends on your solar system size and your charger model.
