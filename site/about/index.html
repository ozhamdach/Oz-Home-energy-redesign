Why Oz Home Energy

An electrician-led energy company

Oz Home Energy designs and installs connected energy systems — solar, batteries, EV charging and the electrical infrastructure behind them — for homes and businesses across Sydney.

Our approach

Solar generates. Batteries store. EV chargers use it. Electrical infrastructure supports all of it.

We think about your property as one connected system, because that's what it actually is — and because the electrical work behind a battery or EV charger is often the part that determines whether a project goes smoothly or runs into problems. We're a licensed electrical contractor first, which shapes how we assess, design and install everything else.

How we work

Four things that shape every project

01

Advice shaped by installation experience

Recommendations come from people who understand how systems perform once they're on the roof and in the switchboard, not just on a spec sheet.

02

Electrical infrastructure considered from the start

Switchboard capacity and wiring condition can affect the final scope of solar, battery and EV charging work.

03

Systems designed around the property

Roof orientation, shading, existing equipment and how you actually use electricity all shape the design, rather than starting from a standard package.

04

Support from enquiry through commissioning and beyond

We stay involved through design, installation, commissioning and any servicing or support requests afterwards.
