## Hard gates

1. **Lead form submits without a fake success confirmation** — **Pass (page has no form of its own).** This page embeds no form; its CTAs link out to `/assessment/`, which carries its own GoHighLevel iframe and its own gate result — out of scope for this page's audit. (The original audit conflated that destination page's iframe with this one — see the Conversion correction above.)
2. **Mobile Performance >= 70, LCP <= 4s** — **Pass.** Lighthouse mobile (simulated throttling): Performance 100/100, LCP 0.6s. (Measured on localhost; real-world network conditions would differ but the margin is large.)
3. **No sideways scroll / horizontal overflow at 375px** — **Pass.** `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` equals `clientWidth` (375px, 0px diff) confirmed programmatically.
4. **NSW electrical contractor licence number shown** — **Pass.** "NSW Electrical Contractor Licence 382607C" appears in the sitewide footer and in the JSON-LD `hasCredential` block.
5. **Accreditation stated correctly (SAA, not CEC)** — **Pass.** "SAA accredited" / "SAA Accreditation" is used consistently in the footer, the JSON-LD schema and the assessment page sidebar. The string "CEC" does not appear anywhere on the page.
6. **Rebate/price/savings claims have an as-of date and cited source** — **Pass.** The only such claim on the page (the STC rebate) is explicitly dated ("Indicative only, as of 24 Sep 2026") and sourced with a link to "Solar Choice — STC Rebate Scheme explained".
7. **No fake/unattributed testimonials, no stock photo presented as real installation** — **Pass.** There are no customer testimonials with star ratings on this page to misattribute. The one quote present is clearly attributed to "Oz, Founder" (not a customer testimonial). The "Recent solar work" gallery photos read as genuine field photography, not stock imagery.
8. **No broken links, no console errors, title+meta present** — **Pass.** All 28 internal links on the page return HTTP 200. Title and meta description are present and unique. The only console errors recorded (`ERR_CERT_AUTHORITY_INVALID` for Google Fonts, `ERR_TUNNEL_CONNECTION_FAILED` for the GoHighLevel form embed on `/assessment/`) trace to this sandboxed test network blocking specific external hosts, not to a defect in the page's own code.
9. **CTA button text has sufficient colour contrast (4.5:1)** — **Pass.** Primary CTA is white text on solid brand blue (`#0560c1`), unambiguous. The hero's secondary "Call" button (white text, white outline over a photo) was flagged by axe as "incomplete" (cannot auto-score text over an image) but manual pixel inspection confirms clear white-on-dark-photo contrast with no legibility issue.

## Steal list

- **Live social proof.** No Google rating or review count appears anywhere on this page. Top Sydney solar competitors lead with "4.9★ on Google, 200+ reviews" pulled live — add it near the hero or trust band.
- **Price and payback transparency.** There is no cost range, no payback-period estimate, and no "what happens on install day" walkthrough anywhere on the page — three of the buyer's most basic questions go unanswered. Competitors publish indicative $ bands per system size (e.g. "$4,000–$7,000 installed for 6.6kW") plus a simple payback estimate.
- **Resilient lead capture (sitewide, not this page specifically).** This page has no form of its own, but its CTA — like every service page's — ultimately routes to `/assessment/`, which embeds a GoHighLevel iframe with no loading state and no error fallback: when it fails to load (ad-blockers, privacy extensions and slow networks commonly block these embeds), visitors get a bare broken-image icon. Worth fixing once, on `/assessment/`, rather than per service page.
- **Suburb-level proof and SEO.** Photo captions and body copy never name a specific suburb — only generic "Sydney". Tagging real jobs ("Rooftop install, Castle Hill NSW") builds hyperlocal trust and captures long-tail suburb search traffic that competitors with suburb-by-suburb case studies already own.
