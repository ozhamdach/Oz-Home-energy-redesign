Common questions, answered plainly

Is a battery right for my existing solar?

It depends on how much solar you're already exporting unused, your evening electricity use, and whether your existing inverter and switchboard can support a battery without major changes. A short assessment of your bill and system details usually answers this before any site visit is needed.

What size solar system suits my home?

System size depends on your roof space and orientation, your daytime electricity use, your electricity retailer's export limit, and your budget. We'd rather size a system to what you'll actually use than sell the largest system your roof can fit.

Can my switchboard support an EV charger?

Many older switchboards don't have available capacity for a dedicated EV circuit without an upgrade. We check this before recommending a charger, and if an upgrade is needed, we'll explain what's involved and why.

What should I know about backup power?

Most battery systems don't back up your entire home automatically — backup circuits are usually a defined subset of essential loads, set up deliberately during installation. It's worth deciding what should stay powered during an outage before your system is designed.

What information is needed for a commercial energy assessment?

Recent electricity bills, your site's approximate roof or ground space, your operating hours, and any known switchboard or supply constraints (such as three-phase capacity). This lets us scope a commercial project enquiry before arranging a site visit.
