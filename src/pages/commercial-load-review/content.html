What we look at

01

When your site actually draws power — the shape of your day decides whether storage is even relevant.

02

Whether you're paying a demand charge — a component priced on your single worst half-hour, not on total use.

03

What your site can physically take — switchboard capacity, protection and available space decide what's installable.

This is for

  • NSW businesses operating from commercial premises
  • Sites with evening, overnight or unpredictable load
  • Sites with solar
  • Sites without solar
  • Businesses reviewing a battery proposal
