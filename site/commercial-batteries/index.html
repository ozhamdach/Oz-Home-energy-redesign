Commercial Battery Storage

Storage for demand management, backup or both

Commercial battery projects are usually driven by one of two goals: reducing peak demand charges, or keeping critical operations running during an outage. The right design depends on which matters more to your business — and often it's both.

Start a Project Enquiry Call 0420 113 216

Common use cases

Why businesses add storage

Peak demand management

Reducing draw from the grid during high-tariff periods by discharging stored energy instead.

Solar self-consumption

Storing excess solar generated outside operating hours or on lower-usage days for later use.

Backup for critical loads

Keeping essential equipment — refrigeration, servers, security systems — running during an outage, where backup functionality is specifically designed and installed for that purpose.

Assessment first

What we need to know

A commercial battery proposal starts with your load profile, existing or planned solar capacity, switchboard and supply arrangement, and which loads matter most during an outage. This is typically gathered through a project enquiry followed by a site assessment.
