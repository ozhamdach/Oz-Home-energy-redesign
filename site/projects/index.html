Projects

Real installations, shown with real detail

Genuine Oz Home Energy installations, published with the property, the system installed, the design decisions behind it and the outcome. No stock photography, no invented figures.

Project write-ups are published here once an installation is complete and the customer has agreed to have it featured. None are published yet — call us and we're happy to talk through recent, similar work directly.

What a project write-up includes

The detail behind every case study

The brief

What the customer was trying to achieve, and the property's roof, orientation and any existing system.

The design

System installed, and the design considerations — shading, switchboard condition, three-phase supply, access or approvals.

The result

Scope and workmanship outcome. Savings or performance figures are only published where they've been measured and the customer has approved sharing them.
