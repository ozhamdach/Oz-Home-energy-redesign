Why the design matters

Panel count isn't the whole story

Two homes with identical roof space can need very different systems. Orientation, shading from trees or neighbouring buildings, your existing switchboard condition, and how much electricity you use during daylight hours all change what actually makes sense.

We start with your electricity bill and your goals — running more of your home on solar during the day, preparing for a future battery, or simply reducing what you draw from the grid — and design from there.

Common questions

Solar, answered plainly

What size solar system suits my home?

It's a balance of available roof space, your daytime electricity use, your retailer's export limit and your budget. We'll talk through options rather than defaulting to the largest system your roof can physically hold.

Do I need council or strata approval?

Most standalone houses don't need council approval for a standard rooftop system, but strata properties, heritage-listed homes and some local overlays can have extra requirements. We'll flag this during your assessment if it applies to you.

Can I add a battery later instead of now?

Yes — many customers install solar first and add a battery once budget allows. We can design the initial system and switchboard with a future battery in mind, so the retrofit is simpler. See solar & battery upgrades.
