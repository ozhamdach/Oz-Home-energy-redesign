Why Oz Home Energy

A modern electrical and energy company, not a solar sales team

Oz Home Energy designs and installs connected energy systems — solar, batteries, EV charging and the electrical infrastructure behind them — for homes and businesses across Sydney and NSW.

Positioning

Solar generates. Batteries store. EV chargers use it. Electrical infrastructure supports all of it.

Most solar companies sell panels. We think about your property as one connected system, because that's what it actually is — and because the electrical work behind a battery or EV charger is often the part that determines whether a project goes smoothly or runs into problems. We're a licensed electrical contractor first, which shapes how we assess, design and install everything else.

How we work

Four things that shape every project

01

Advice shaped by installation experience

Recommendations come from people who understand how systems perform once they're on the roof and in the switchboard, not just on a spec sheet.

02

Electrical infrastructure considered from the start

We check switchboard capacity and wiring condition before quoting solar, battery or EV work — the part that's easy to overlook and expensive to fix later.

03

Systems designed around the property

Roof orientation, shading, existing equipment and how you actually use electricity all shape the design, rather than starting from a standard package.

04

Support from enquiry through commissioning and beyond

One team from the first phone call through design, installation, commissioning and ongoing servicing or support requests.

Licensing & compliance

Licensed NSW Electrical Contractor

Oz Home Energy operates under NSW Electrical Contractor Licence 382607C. All electrical work — including solar, battery and EV installations — is carried out by licensed electricians to Australian Standards, with compliance documentation provided.
