All frequently asked questions

Want the full workings? Read our federal battery rebate guide or how solar payback is actually calculated.

What size solar system suits my home?

System size is a balance of your roof space and orientation, how much electricity you use during daylight hours, your DNSP-approved export limit, and your budget. We work from your electricity bill and household routine first, rather than sizing purely to "fill the roof."

Can my switchboard support an EV charger or a battery?

Many older switchboards don't have available capacity without an upgrade — this is one of the first things we check, before discussing which equipment to install.

Do I need council or strata approval for solar?

Most standalone houses don't need council approval for a standard rooftop system. Strata properties, heritage-listed homes and some local planning overlays can have extra requirements — we'll flag this during your assessment if it's likely to apply.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — Oz Home Energy holds a NSW Electrical Contractor Licence. Insurance details are available on request.

What areas do you service?

Residential and commercial work is focused on Sydney and Greater Sydney, NSW. See service areas.

What if something goes wrong after installation?

Oz Home Energy's installation workmanship is covered by a 10-year workmanship warranty. Manufacturer product warranties remain separate. Eligibility, exclusions and the claim process are set out in the written warranty terms provided with the applicable quotation and contract. Nothing in this warranty limits rights available under the Australian Consumer Law. To request assistance, submit a service request.
