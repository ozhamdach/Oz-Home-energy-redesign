Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026.

1. Who we are

Electrical Hub Pty Ltd (ABN 72 665 477 556), trading as Oz Home Energy ("we", "us", "our"), is a licensed NSW electrical contractor that designs and installs solar, battery and EV charging systems. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and how you can access or correct it.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you deal with us, we may collect:

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email address, postcode and property address.
  • Enquiry details: what you asked us to check or quote (for example a switchboard, existing solar, a battery or an EV charger), whether you own the property, and anything you send us such as electricity bills or photos.
  • Quote and job records: quotes and proposals, inspection notes and photos, and installation, warranty and payment records.
  • Website and advertising data: your IP address, device and browser, pages visited, and which ad or link brought you to us, collected through cookies and similar tools, including from Meta and Google.
  • Messages: emails, texts, calls and messages you send us, including through Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp.

3. How we collect it

Directly from you, when you complete a form on our website, submit a Facebook or Instagram lead form, call, text, email or message us, or meet us at your home. Automatically, through cookies and analytics tools on our website. From Meta, which passes us the details you enter into a lead form.

4. How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, book an inspection and prepare your quote; to design, install and maintain your system and handle warranty claims; to complete paperwork for rebates, finance and compliance where you've asked us to; to follow up about your enquiry; to measure and improve our advertising; and to meet our legal and licensing obligations.

5. Marketing messages

We contact you about your enquiry by phone, SMS and email. We send marketing messages only with your consent, and every marketing message includes a way to opt out. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to a text or contacting us.

6. How we store and share information

We share personal information only as needed, with:

  • Service providers that help us run the business — customer management and messaging (HighLevel), quoting software (GreenSketch), email (Gmail), and advertising and analytics platforms (Meta, Google).
  • Installers and subcontractors who work on your job.
  • Government bodies, accreditation bodies and finance providers, only where needed for a rebate, loan or compliance process you've asked us to complete.
  • Anyone else we're required to by law.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

7. Overseas storage

Some of our service providers store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States. We take reasonable steps to make sure they handle it securely.

8. Security and how long we keep it

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, including restricting access within our systems to those who need it to do their job. We keep information for as long as we need it for the purposes above, including warranty and record-keeping obligations, then delete or de-identify it.

9. Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we're allowed to, by contacting us using the details below. If you have a concern about how we've handled your personal information, see our complaints process. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au if you feel your concern hasn't been resolved.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated.
