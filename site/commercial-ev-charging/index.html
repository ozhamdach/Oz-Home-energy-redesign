Commercial EV Charging

Charging infrastructure for staff, fleet or customers

Multiple chargers on a commercial site raise different questions to a single home charger — available supply capacity, load management across units, and whether charging is for your own fleet, staff vehicles or customers.

Questions we work through

Scoping a commercial EV project

Who is charging?

Fleet vehicles, staff parking or public/customer access each change charger type, access control and billing needs.

How much supply is available?

Existing three-phase capacity often determines how many chargers a site can support without an upgrade.

Load management

Dynamic load management can allow more chargers to share available capacity without exceeding site supply limits.

Solar integration

Where a site has or is adding commercial solar, charging can be aligned with generation where practical.

Access & billing

Options for restricting access to authorised vehicles, or metering usage for cost recovery, depending on the equipment chosen.

Staged rollout

Infrastructure can be installed to support future charger additions without redoing supply work later.
